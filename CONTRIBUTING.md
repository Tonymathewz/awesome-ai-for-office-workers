# Contributing

Thanks for helping office workers find good AI tools.

## What belongs here

- Tools, guides, prompts or skills that help with **everyday office work**: email, spreadsheets, documents, PDFs, scheduling, bookings, reports.
- Usable by **non-programmers** (or with a clear beginner guide).
- **Free**, or with a real free option.
- Actively maintained, or still clearly useful.

## What doesn't belong

- Tools only developers can use.
- Paid-only products, affiliate links or advertising.
- Anything that collects or exposes personal data without a clear reason.

## How to add something

1. Add it to the right section, at the end of the list.
2. Use this format:
   `- [Name](link) - What it does for an office worker, in one sentence.`
3. Keep descriptions short and plain. No marketing words.
4. Open a pull request saying why you recommend it.

Fake sample data only, please. Never real customer names, phone numbers or company records.
